The Chase Sapphire Preferred® Card has long been considered the gold standard for everyday travelers seeking outsized value from a mid-tier travel credit card. With a modest $95 annual fee and access to Chase's best-in-class transfer partners, it consistently earns its spot in millions of wallets.

The current welcome offer of 80,000 Chase Ultimate Rewards® points after spending $4,000 in the first three months is worth approximately $1,640 based on TPG's latest valuations — more than 17x the annual fee in the first year alone.

Where this card truly shines is in its transfer partner ecosystem. Chase partners with 14 airlines and hotels, including United, Hyatt, and Singapore Airlines — giving cardholders the flexibility to maximize points across multiple programs. Hyatt transfers in particular offer some of the best redemption value in the industry, with top-tier properties available for 35,000–45,000 points per night.

The card earns 3x points on dining and online grocery purchases, 3x on streaming services, 2x on all other travel, and 1x on everything else. A $50 annual hotel credit further offsets the annual fee. For most households, this structure comfortably covers the annual fee without ever touching the welcome bonus.

For travelers who want flexibility without paying a premium annual fee, the Sapphire Preferred strikes the right balance. It's the card we recommend most often to first-time travel rewards enthusiasts — and one that experienced travelers frequently keep even after upgrading to premium cards.
